## Former primary target: ACM IUI 2027

IUI is a CCF-B fit by classification, but it is no longer the scientific
primary target for this version. The seventh CCF list places IUI in the
human-computer interaction and pervasive-computing B tier, and counts full or
regular conference papers rather than posters, demos, short papers, or
workshops. The IUI 2027 call explicitly includes human-centered recommendation,
user modelling, personalization, human control in daily automation, LLMs and
agentic AI, human-agent interaction, and reproducibility.

- Abstract deadline: 13 August 2026, AoE.
- Full-paper deadline: 20 August 2026, AoE.
- Review: double blind.
- Evidence can be computational analysis when appropriate to the claims.
- At least one accepted-paper author must register, attend in person, and
  present.

Official sources:

- <https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/HCIAndPC/>
- <https://iui.acm.org/2027/call-for-papers/>

## Independent submission

The call does not require a professor, supervisor, or senior corresponding
author. It defines the corresponding author as the person who submits the
paper, not necessarily the first author. A single-author submission is
therefore allowed by the published conference rules. This does not override
any separate university policy, research-ethics requirement, funding rule, or
visa/attendance constraint that may apply to the author.

## Submission decision

IUI 2027 is the primary target only if the following are complete by the paper
deadline:

1. GRU-family robustness gates pass or the paper transparently reports the
   boundary condition.
2. The paid LLM pilot passes action-validity and timeout gates and yields enough
   information to justify a confirmatory sample, or the paper is explicitly
   framed as a deterministic-agent computational study without LLM claims.
3. The paper contains an anonymized artifact package, model/prompt provenance,
   negative cases, uncertainty, and a practical/societal-impact reflection.

The deadline is unusually close. The scientific no-go is submitting an
underpowered LLM result merely to mention LLMs. A clean deterministic-agent plus
two-recommender paper is preferable to an invalid LLM appendix.

## Conditional primary target: ACM RecSys 2027

The current seventh CCF list places RecSys in database/data-mining/content
retrieval B. Its 2027 main-track dates are not yet published as of this review.
RecSys 2026 used an April abstract deadline and required relevant executable
artifacts for review, so a 2027 submission offers materially more time for a
full LLM-agent study and stronger recommender-family evaluation.
