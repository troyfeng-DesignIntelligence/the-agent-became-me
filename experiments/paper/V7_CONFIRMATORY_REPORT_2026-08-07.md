# V7 confirmatory instrument ablation

The complete natural-frame study passed the frozen treatment-meaning gate.
Across 460 tasks crossed with SASRec and GRU4Rec, changing only the order of the
same candidate records produced a categorical energy distance of 0.193 [0.155,
0.233] for the primary DeepSeek model. The byte-identical masked control was
0.004 [-0.003, 0.013]. MiMo reproduced the direction: served order 0.147 [0.115,
0.183], masked control 0.000 [-0.007, 0.008].

Both providers completed 14,720 valid calls with no failures or timeouts. Every
cross-arm change was a selected-candidate swap; none crossed between selection
and abandonment. DeepSeek cost $1.756 and MiMo cost CNY 12.266 at the frozen
price schedule.

The result closes the instrument-meaning P0 for categorical provider outputs in
this research storefront: an order-only representation survives after explicit
rank and inspection metadata are removed. It does not establish human behavior,
purchase quality, regret, production-retailer effects, or long-term harm. The
consequential-outcome P0 remains open.
