# Final report

The four-category replication passed every frozen evidence gate (`1.000`).
Twelve SASRec checkpoints were evaluated over 30,838 histories and 261,836
retained events. All four category-specific persist-all 95% intervals were
positive; the equal-category estimate was +0.321 [0.273, 0.369], and every
leave-one-category-out lower bound remained positive.

The effect was not constant: estimates ranged from +0.119 to +0.612. The paper
and website therefore frame the result as recurrence with domain heterogeneity.
