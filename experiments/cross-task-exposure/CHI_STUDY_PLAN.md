# Cross-Task Contamination Study Plan

## Phenomenon and stake

A delegated shopping task may be temporary, other-directed, or abandoned while its interaction trace is persistent. The consequential interaction question is not merely whether a profile changes, but whether that trace reaches later recommendation surfaces before a person or agent can correct it.

## Unit and comparisons

The unit is a paired synthetic task sequence: one temporary task followed by eight rounds of unrelated personal shopping. Each scenario and seed is replayed under persist-all, exact target deduplication, intent weighting, outcome quarantine, rollback on failure, and commit-only scope gating.

## Outcomes

- temporary-topic recommendation slots during four provisional surfaces;
- temporary-topic slots during eight post-outcome follow-up rounds;
- number of contaminated follow-up rounds and first clean round;
- share of slots serving the current personal task.

## Alternative explanations controlled

Conditions share the catalog, temporary events, follow-up task, ranking weights, seed, item quality, and tie-breaking jitter. The manipulation is the lifecycle of the same evidence. The hand-authored recommender remains an external-validity limitation.

## Claim boundary

The study can establish properties of the executable controlled recommender. It cannot establish commercial-platform prevalence, human notice, experienced harm, or preferred controls without separate platform and human evidence.
