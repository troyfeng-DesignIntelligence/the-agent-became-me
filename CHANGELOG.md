# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-05

### Added

- Research-owned online shopping storefront with immediate and held activation arms.
- Eight-task browser-agent pilot with four lifecycle snapshots per arm.
- Mechanical evaluator for fixed traces, evidence isolation, matched resolved states, public-output privacy, and evidence integrity.
- Public redacted evidence timelines and activation-timing results.
- GitHub Pages deployment workflow, custom 404 page, sitemap, robots policy, and production CSP.

### Changed

- Reframed visible terminology around delegated-evidence activation, staged holding, and task-boundary exposure.
- Added a direct link from the main research narrative to the online research storefront.

### Boundaries

- The storefront and recommender are research-owned; no third-party commerce recommender is manipulated.
- The agent cannot access accounts, carts, checkout, payment, or external product pages.
